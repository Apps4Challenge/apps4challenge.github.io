Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the publisher of Qubix3D - Arrow Cube Puzzle ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you use the mobile game (the "Game") and this website (together, the "Services"). We respect your privacy and collect as little information as possible.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide. The Game does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address or phone number. If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the content of your message.

Information collected automatically. When you use the Game, we and our third-party partners may automatically collect technical information such as device model, operating system version, language, approximate location derived from your IP address, advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Google Advertising ID on Android), app version, gameplay events (for example, levels completed) and crash or performance diagnostics.

Game progress. Your progress and settings are stored locally on your device and may be backed up by your device's operating system.

2. How we use information

  • To provide, maintain and improve the Game, and to fix bugs and crashes;
  • To show advertisements, including personalized advertisements where you have given consent, and to measure their performance;
  • To process in-app purchases and restore them (purchases are handled by Apple or Google; we do not receive your payment card details);
  • To understand how the Game is used through aggregated statistics;
  • To respond to your requests and to comply with legal obligations.

3. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

We process personal data on the basis of: your consent (for example, for personalized advertising and, where required, analytics); our legitimate interests in operating, securing and improving the Game; the performance of our contract with you; and compliance with legal obligations. You may withdraw your consent at any time from the in-game privacy settings or your device settings.

4. Advertising and tracking

The Game may use third-party advertising and analytics services, which may include Google AdMob and other mediation partners. These partners may collect and use information as described in their own privacy policies. On iOS, we will only access your advertising identifier for tracking if you allow it through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. In the EEA and the UK, a consent message is shown before personalized ads are served. You can also reset your advertising identifier or limit ad personalization in your device settings. If you decline, you may still see non-personalized ads.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers that help us operate the Game (such as advertising, analytics, crash reporting and hosting providers), with Apple or Google for app distribution and purchases, when required by law, or in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets.

6. International transfers

Our partners may process information in countries other than your own. When personal data from the EEA or the UK is transferred, appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses are used.

7. Data retention

We keep information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law. Emails you send us are kept as long as needed to handle your request. Data stored locally is deleted when you uninstall the Game.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. California residents may request information about, and deletion of, their personal information, and have the right not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. To exercise your rights, contact us at contact@mountilium.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9. Data deletion

Because the Game does not use accounts, most information is stored only on your device and can be deleted by uninstalling the Game. To request deletion of any information we or our partners may hold, email us at contact@mountilium.com and, if possible, include your advertising identifier.

10. Children

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

12. This website

This website does not use cookies or tracking scripts. It only stores your language choice in your browser's local storage. Our hosting provider may record standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security purposes.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

14. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at contact@mountilium.com.
